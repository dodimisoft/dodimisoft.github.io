Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. GENERAL

This privacy policy explains what data is processed when you use the Castle Go mobile game (the "Game"). The Game is developed and published by DoDiMi; "we" in this policy means DoDiMi. DoDiMi is the controller of your data and can be reached at dodimisoft@gmail.com.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

a) Game progress. The levels you finish, your stars, your gold and gem balances, the weapons you unlock, the medals you earn and your settings. This data is stored on your device and in your cloud save. When you first open the game you get an anonymous user ID; even without signing in, your progress is saved in the cloud under that ID.

b) Player name. When you start, you get a random player name such as "Player A4H7B393"; you can change it in Settings at any time.

c) Account details. If you sign in with Google or Apple, the provider sends us a user ID and your email address. Your email address is shown only to you in Settings and never to other players. We never see or store your password.

d) Purchase data. When you make an in-app purchase, the store (App Store or Google Play) verifies the transaction. Your card details never reach us; we are only told that the purchase went through.

3. HOW WE USE DATA

We process data only to: run the game and save your progress to the cloud; sync it across devices when you are signed in; build the leaderboards; and verify and restore purchases.

Legal basis: your game progress, player name, account details, leaderboard and purchase data are necessary to provide the Game to you and are processed to perform our terms of use (the contract). Data for personalised advertising is processed only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

4. LEADERBOARDS

If you sign in, your score and player name become visible to other players on the leaderboard; your email address does not. If you would rather not take part, you can play as a guest without signing in; your score is then not visible to other players.

5. ADVERTISING

The Game includes optional rewarded video ads. Watching them is entirely up to you; you can keep playing without ever watching one. Ad providers may use their own cookies and identifiers.

6. SHARING

We do not sell your data and we do not hand it to third parties for marketing. Data is shared only with these providers that run the service: Google Firebase (cloud saves, authentication and leaderboards), Google Play and the App Store (purchase verification) and our advertising provider (rewarded video ads).

These providers may process data on servers outside your country. Such transfers take place under the standard contractual clauses and security measures the providers apply.

7. RETENTION AND DELETION

Your cloud data is kept for as long as your account exists. If you play as a guest, your save is tied to the app on this device; if you uninstall it, the save can no longer be reached. "Delete Account" in Settings permanently removes your account, all cloud progress and your leaderboard entries. This cannot be undone.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The Game does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we find that such data has reached us, we delete it.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to access your data, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, to object to processing, and to take your data elsewhere. To use these rights, write to dodimisoft@gmail.com. If the game is no longer installed on your device, you can also ask for your account and data to be deleted by emailing the same address with the subject "Account deletion"; include the email address of the Google account you signed in with.

10. CHANGES

This policy may be updated from time to time. You will be told inside the game when something important changes.

11. CONTACT

Send your questions to dodimisoft@gmail.com.
