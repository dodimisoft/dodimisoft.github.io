Delete your Castle Go account

You can delete your Castle Go account and all data linked to it at any time.

If the game is installed

Open the game → Settings → Delete Account, and confirm twice. Deletion happens immediately.

If the game is not installed

Email dodimisoft@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion" and include the email address of the Google account you signed in with. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account, your cloud save (levels, stars, medals, diamonds and premium items) and your leaderboard entries. Nothing is kept.

Guest players have no account linked to an email; a guest save is tied to the app on that device and is removed when the game is uninstalled.

A No Ads purchase belongs to your store account, not to the game account: it can be restored from the store after reinstalling.
